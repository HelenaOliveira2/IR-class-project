## Field Explanations

- **Development Effort (0-5):** Contribution to the core development and technical aspects of the project.
- **Documentation & Reporting (0-5):** Effort in writing the project report, documentation, and other written materials.
- **Team Collaboration & Communication (0-5):** Contribution to team discussions, helping other members, and effective communication.
- **Research & Idea Contribution (0-5):** Effort in researching new ideas, finding solutions to problems, and contributing to the project's conceptualization.
- **Comments/Justification:** A brief explanation for the scores given.
